Turning ocean waste into value.

Modular biorefineries transforming aquaculture by-products into high-value chitosan and bioactive peptides.

The Problem

Valuable ocean resources are being wasted.

For small and medium aquaculture producers, waste management is a cost center. High-value biomaterials remain locked in shells and offal, creating environmental burden instead of circular revenue.

Disposal Costs

Producers pay to remove shells and trimmings, a recurring expense with zero recovery.

Environmental Load

Untreated waste increases emissions and nutrient pressure on coastal ecosystems.

The Solution

Decentralized blue bioprocessing.

Our modular units bring high-end lab processing directly to the source—farms and coastal hubs—converting shrimp shells and fish offal into marketable bioproducts.

Technology

Modular, IoT-enabled hardware.

Standardized Yield

Built for reliability. Our system ensures high-quality chitosan and peptide output every cycle, giving partners confidence in their end-products.

IoT

Real-time Monitoring

Embedded sensors track temperature, pH, and flow timing to maintain perfect processing conditions automatically.

Scalable

From single units to multi-unit hubs, the hardware grows with the producer's needs.

AI-Assisted

Machine learning models optimize processing variables to maximize yield from different species and waste qualities.

Validation

Proven Bench-Scale Prototype.

AquaCircle has moved beyond theory. We are currently validating our modular workflow using standard lab equipment to simulate the full-scale unit's efficiency.
